# Selected CIRAW renditions

The two JPEGs are full-resolution, orientation-corrected renditions of the
[parent RAW originals](../README.md), selected during the camera highlight
comparison. They preserve the chosen neutral CIRAW reconstruction target; the
camera JPEGs are independent renditions, not ground truth.

[Provenance](provenance.json) records the generating host, binary fingerprints,
image dimensions and output hashes. Its orientation value describes normalized
pixel orientation; these JPEGs have no EXIF orientation tag. Highlight
reconstruction was enabled; the
helper's automatic tone boosts and baseline exposure were zero. No additional
highlight compression or development preset was applied. Decoder updates may
change exact pixels, so use
these for visual comparison rather than byte-exact cross-platform tests.
