# 07 — A durable evaluation reference collection

The user's requested deliverable is an organized, reusable image collection,
not a new evaluation service. Keep its index under `fixtures/segmentation/`,
reachable from the fixture hub. Reuse the permanent camera originals in place.

Separate photographed scene from rendering: RAW, camera JPEG and our developed
PNG may depict the same scene but cannot be substituted in pixel comparisons.
Retain the exact research source images, selection intent, coordinates, source
provenance and reviewed outputs outside temporary directories.

Separate visual targets from measured alpha truth and from candidate outputs.
The PhotoLab screenshot and video overlay are visual targets with different
framing/grading and no exported alpha. Our preferred masks remain model outputs,
never ideal labels. Future human-authored or exported ideal masks can be added
with explicit alignment and provenance. Unknown rights remain unknown; retaining
the collection does not authorize public redistribution.

Organize the existing camera scenes by photographic content and name their
useful selection challenges. Mark those not yet evaluated honestly. Preserve
the portrait and landscape experiments used in this task, including their
different intents and known defects. Keep synthetic cardinality diagnostics
distinct from independent photographs.

Verify retained copies against their source hashes, check dimensions and links,
and inspect representative previews. No new model calls, threshold fitting,
benchmark framework or duplicate multi-gigabyte RAW collection is needed.
