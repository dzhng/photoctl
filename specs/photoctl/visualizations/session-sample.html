C · The prebuilt prompts

Under the two-bucket rule these are the generative features. Placeholders in {braces}.

C1

fill --remove

Remove {subject_or_"the masked object"} from the masked region and reconstruct what would be behind it. Continue the surrounding background exactly: same perspective, light direction, color temperature, depth of field, and film grain. Add no new objects, people, or text. Change nothing outside the mask.

Sent with: the crop + --pad ring, the mask (native or drawn as a hint), seed. Grain matching matters on ISO 3200 wedding frames.

C2

fill --outpaint

Extend this photograph beyond its current edges into the transparent border. Continue the scene as the same lens would have seen it: same horizon line, perspective, lighting, and grain. Keep every existing pixel unchanged. Do not introduce new subjects.

photoctl pads the canvas locally to the target --aspect or --px and marks the pad as the mask; the model never chooses dimensions.

C3

relight — a prompt template, drift documented

Relight this portrait as if a single soft studio key light were placed at {azimuth}° azimuth (0 = camera-right, 90 = directly above) and {elevation}° elevation with intensity {intensity} of 1. Keep the person's identity, expression, pose, clothing, and the background unchanged. Adjust only illumination, shadow direction, and specular highlights.

Whole-frame regeneration through reimagine's path. Output carries "drift":"full-frame" so agents don't mistake it for a local edit.

C4

auto_enhance — VLM proposes develop keys

You are a careful photo editor. Given this image (a 1024px preview) and its measured stats {p02, p50, p98, clipped_hi_pct, clipped_lo_pct, mean_sat, est_wb_k}, return only JSON with keys from: exposure [-2,2], highlights, shadows, contrast, black_point, vibrance, saturation [-100,100], white_balance.temp_offset_k [-1500,1500]. Be conservative. Protect skin tones. Never clip highlights that are currently held. Explain nothing.

Result is written as ordinary develop keys — inspectable in show, undoable with --reset, never a black box.
