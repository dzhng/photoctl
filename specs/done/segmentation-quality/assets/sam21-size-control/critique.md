# Small versus Large: boundary review

Verdict: neither size is accepted as PhotoLab-quality detailed selection. Both
locate the broad subjects, with no consistent quality winner across boundaries.

An unprimed reviewer inspected the three paired full overlays and masks, plus
all fixed overlay/mask crops for both sizes: five hair features and three landscape
features for each sky/road state. Parent inspection independently agrees with the
main defects. The reviewer subsequently inspected all twelve selected black/white
composites and all eighteen retained candidate masks. Full landscape composites
were displayed at reduced size; fixed crops provide the detailed evidence, not
an exhaustive native-pixel audit. A is Small and B is Large in the retained pairs.

| Feature | Less wrong | Visible reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hair crown | Mixed | Small retains fragmented detail and holes; Large merges background gaps into a smoother solid silhouette. Both miss outer curls. |
| Hair right curl | Large for coverage | Large includes more of the projecting dark curl but fills surrounding background and misses finer strands. |
| Hair face curl | Mixed | Large retains more curl and excludes eyebrow specks, but also selects skin between strands. |
| Hair bottom | Both wrong | Long visible hanging curls are omitted; Small's extra tuft does not trace individual hairs. |
| Flowers | Tie | Both exclude the inspected crop and broadly separate flowers and face. |
| Sky foliage | Both wrong | Blue margins around crowns and internal sky gaps remain unselected. |
| Sky wires | Mixed | Large excludes more wire length but removes wide bands of valid sky; some wires remain selected in both. |
| Road bend | Rough tie | Both locate the curve; neither precisely follows grass intrusions. |
| Road continuation | Small | Large ends prematurely near the fence, missing visible pavement. |
| Road gravel junction | Large | Small selects disconnected gravel patches; Large excludes them more cleanly. |

The full composites additionally reveal a thin unselected strip along the top
sky boundary and retained distant wires at the far-right horizon in both selected
sky masks. Some unused candidates have substantial semantic failures (person
regions in hair, forest in sky, grass in road); none justifies visually substituting
an alternative after selection was frozen.

Large is therefore not a drop-in solution for the observed gap. This rejects this
specific size-upgrade hypothesis, not every possible SAM 2.1 prompting or refinement
pipeline. The original first critic failed before delivering a review; this record
uses the completed replacement review, not a presumed result from that failed run.
