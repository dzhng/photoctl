# Keyless relight journey

Deterministic built-CLI evidence for the Slice 13a C3 relight specialization. `source.png` is the
imported fixture, `before.png` is its exposure-adjusted current render, `after.png` is the result of
azimuth 35, elevation 60, and intensity 0.75, and `restored.png` is the render after removing the
relight layer.

All captures are 160×120 opaque PNGs. `before.png` and `restored.png` are byte-identical. The fake
gateway proves command, prompt, graph, density, and restoration contracts; it is not evidence of a
live model's photographic lighting quality.

The comparison gate measured a material full-frame change (grayscale MAE 58.49, distance 0.653).
The candidate retained 492 color buckets, 93.77 luminance contrast, and no transparency; the first
opaque-mask attempt rendered as a black void and was rejected. Mapping C3 intensity to the shared
whole-frame blend produced the accepted deterministic candidate while preserving the versioned
provider guidance.

A fresh unprimed critique accepted the checkpoint with high confidence: the candidate is coherent,
opaque, and full-frame, with aligned geometry and no void, corruption, seam, framing shift, or partial
application. It noted that the synthetic gradient cannot establish photographic detail preservation;
that remains live-model evidence rather than a keyless command-contract gate.
